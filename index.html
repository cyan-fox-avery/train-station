The Train StationPrototype 1 — v0.1.1

A small, abandoned rural Canadian station. No quest markers, no narrator, no score. Look around, try things, and see what changes.

Sound needs one tap on iPad/iPhone before it can play. The train will pass shortly after you begin, not before.

The platform

Weathered siding, ivy, dandelions through cracked concrete. A faded timetable. A switch house. And, on its pole above the platform, the clock — stopped.

The clock is stopped.

🚂 🚃 🚃 🚃

The track is quiet.

The waiting-room door is shut, ivy-draped, with leaves drifted against its threshold.

The waiting room

You are inside. It smells faintly of old wood and rain. Light comes through the cleared window.

Faded arrivals board: the writing is worn, but you can still make out times that no longer run.

Lost-and-found shelf: one single glove, a little dusty. Nobody came back for it.

Maintenance notice: “Platform drainage — water pooling near the platform edge.” That is the next loop. It is not in Prototype 1.

You arrive on foot. The station is quiet. What catches your eye first?
What you have: Nothing yet — just your hands.
Door: Shut. Hinge seized, threshold blocked.
Prototype 1 deliberately has no inventory economy, no ending screen, and no second train. The train passes once, early, and then it is gone.

What to test: Did you notice the problem without being told? Did trying the door make sense? Did you want to look around afterwards? That is the whole test.
